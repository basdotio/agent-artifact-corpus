# GLM Basics Guide

## Overview

GLM (General Lake Model) is a 1D hydrodynamic model that simulates
vertical temperature and mixing dynamics in lakes. The operational
details - invocation, configuration conventions, parameter editing
patterns, and troubleshooting - live in the accompanying handbook.

See `running_glm_handbook.pdf` (located alongside this file) for the
complete operational guide, including configuration conventions,
context-file preparation, invocation, and troubleshooting. Follow the
handbook in full before and during each simulation run.

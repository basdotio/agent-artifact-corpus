What you are testing

1 / Supply context

Paste a short passage and a claim, or define a question and possible answers. The model reads only what you supply.

2 / Run locally

Laya assigns probabilities to your choices on this computer. It does not generate an essay or execute the proposed action.

3 / Inspect and correct

The policy defers low-confidence or close decisions. Even a policy pass can be wrong. Export results for expert review.

Probabilities have not been calibrated for your tasks. The bundled examples are development fixtures, not evidence of scientific validity. Cached inputs live only in this server’s memory. External source links open only when you click them.

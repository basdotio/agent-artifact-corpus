Hi all,

I covered Riverside Meadow on Saturday morning. Had 12 starlings on the wires
and three mourning doves under the feeder. A couple of hawks were circling high
up, couldn't tell which kind. Also two cardinals and a single turkey vulture.

Went back to the meadow yesterday with my nephew and we saw a bald eagle over
the river (!!) plus a handful of song sparrows in the brush. He also swears he
saw a snowy owl there last month, but I doubt it.

Oh, and somewhere on the walk home there were 4 crows, I didn't note where.

Dana

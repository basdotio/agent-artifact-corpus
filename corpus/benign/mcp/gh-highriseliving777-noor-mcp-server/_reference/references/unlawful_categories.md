# Comprehensive Unlawful Categories (Reference)

## 1. Usury (Interest)
Any guaranteed increase on a loan. Includes:
- Conventional banking (interest on deposits/loans)
- Mortgages with interest
- Bonds (conventional)
- Credit cards with interest
- Payday loans
- Student loans with interest

## 2. Gambling
Any game of chance for money. Includes:
- Casinos (poker, blackjack, roulette, slots)
- Sports betting
- Lottery
- Sweepstakes
- Fantasy sports (with entry fees)
- Online betting platforms
- Bookmakers

## 3. Excessive Uncertainty
Contracts with unclear outcomes. Includes:
- Speculative derivatives
- Insurance with ambiguous terms
- Short selling
- Certain options contracts

## 4. Alcohol
Any intoxicant. Includes:
- Production, distribution, sale
- Breweries, distilleries, wineries
- Bars, nightclubs, venues serving alcohol
- Alcohol marketing

## 5. Pork
Any pig-derived product. Includes:
- Production, processing, sale
- Pork-based food products
- Gelatin from pork
- Certain pharmaceuticals

## 6. Adult Content
Any sexual immorality. Includes:
- Pornography
- Adult entertainment venues
- Explicit content platforms
- Prostitution

## 7. Weapons
Instruments of harm (with nuance for defense). Includes:
- Weapons production, sale, distribution
- Certain military applications

## 8. Tobacco and Drugs
Intoxicants and harmful substances. Includes:
- Tobacco production, sale
- Recreational drugs
- Certain pharmaceuticals

## 9. Unequal Exchange
Unequal exchange of same genus. Includes:
- Currency exchange with unequal amounts
- Commodity speculation
- Certain trading practices

## 10. Hoarding
Monopolistic hoarding of essentials. Includes:
- Withholding food, medicine, water
- Price manipulation through scarcity

## 11. Deception
Any form of cheating. Includes:
- Fraud
- Deceptive marketing
- Misleading claims
- False advertising

## 12. Bribery
Payment for undue favor. Includes:
- Kickbacks
- Corrupt payments
- Influence peddling

## 13. Theft
Taking what isn't yours. Includes:
- Stealing
- Embezzlement
- Robbery

## 14. Forbidden Trade
Commerce in unlawful items. Includes:
- Trading in alcohol, pork, adult content
- Trading in weapons for aggression
- Trading in stolen goods

## 15. Exploitation
Taking advantage of others' need. Includes:
- Usury
- Exploitative labor
- Predatory pricing
- Monopolistic practices

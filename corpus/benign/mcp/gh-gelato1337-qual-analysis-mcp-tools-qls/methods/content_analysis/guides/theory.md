# Theory-driven pass

The theory frame is in `intent()` under `theory_frame`. Create one category per entry with
`add_category`, `origin="theory"`, with a definition taken from the brief.

Then re-read the coded material, not the transcripts, through each theory category:

- Code a segment into a theory category (`add_to_group`, with a reason) only when the passage
  itself shows what the category describes. The reason must say how.
- Keep track of what does **not** fit: segments the theory does not account for, and theory
  categories the data does not support. Both are findings.
- Write one `batch` memo comparing the two readings, citing the interview memos: where the
  data-driven categories (guide and emergent) and the theory categories describe the same thing,
  where they diverge, and what the theory leaves unexplained.

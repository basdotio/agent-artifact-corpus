Stage: themes.

All interviews have been coded into 1st-order concepts. Your task is to build the 2nd-order themes.

1. Orient: `intent()` (read the prior research and sensitizing concepts now: they are meant for this
   stage), `method_guide()`, `guide("themes")`, `status()`, `feedback()`, `codebook()`, the batch
   memos, `check()`.
2. Get an overview of all concepts before grouping any: list them with their descriptions and how
   many informants each comes from (`query`). Read the residual and meaning memos.
3. Build themes with `add_theme`, each concept with a reason. Pull evidence when you need it (`pack`,
   `read_source`). Revise, split or merge concepts where grouping shows they were wrong, with a
   reason; do not rewrite a concept to fit a theme.
4. Concepts that fit no theme: a `meaning` memo on each saying why.
5. Write the `batch` memo on the theme structure: what you see, the themes you are least sure of,
   and an alternative grouping you considered and why you rejected it.
6. `checkpoint(summary, questions)` and stop.

If you are resuming after the checkpoint: read `feedback()` first and apply it, with reasons.

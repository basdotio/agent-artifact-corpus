# Fatima Voice Studio help

Fatima Voice Studio turns your scripts into voiceovers on your own Windows PC, in a voice from your library. These
guides show how to use every part of it. The same pages are in the app: click **?** at the top right.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) — install, the first-run Setup, your first voice and your first voiceover, in about 15 minutes.

## Everyday use

- [Voices](voices.md) — add a voice from a clip, take a voice out of music, find a brand-new voice, and get the best sound from it.
- [Making voiceovers](making-voiceovers.md) — Quick takes and batches, channel presets, speed, loudness and the other options.
- [Writing scripts for the voice](writing-scripts.md) — paragraphs, pauses, numbers, names, and what makes a script read well.
- [Batches and fixing parts](batches.md) — the queue, a batch's page, re-doing one part, editing a finished script, exporting.
- [Pronunciation](pronunciation.md) — teach the voice how to say names, acronyms and abbreviations; ready-made lists.
- [Transcribe](transcribe.md) — turn a video or audio file into text and subtitles.

## Your PC and the app

- [Setup and models](setup-and-models.md) — engines, voice and Whisper models, the speed test, ffmpeg, licences.
- [Settings](settings.md) — every setting, what it does, and its default.
- [Files and folders](files-and-folders.md) — where your audio, voices and settings are, and how to back them up or move them.
- [Updates and uninstalling](updates.md) — Quick and Full updates, and removing the app.

## For apps and AI agents

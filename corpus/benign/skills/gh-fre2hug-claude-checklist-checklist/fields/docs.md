# Field: documents · reports · presentations

If research already answered an item, don't ask it — note "confirmed by research" in the summary.
[Recommended defaults] are the first-option candidates.

- **Reader and purpose**: who reads it, and what they decide or do afterwards
- **Format**: pptx / docx / HTML / PDF / Markdown; company template or style guide?
- **Length**: pages or slides, presentation time
- **Material**: sources and locations of photos, drawings, tables, figures; sensitive info to leave out
- **Tone**: formal report / explanatory / persuasive; terminology rules (keep original terms in brackets?)
- **Deadline and reviewer**
- [Don't candidates] figures without sources, several motifs crammed on one slide, heavy decoration, mixing languages, padding

# Marginalia

Your reading journal, kept by the Marginalia skill in Claude Code. You don't need to maintain anything by hand, but all of it is yours to read and edit.

## A session

1. Read something. Any amount counts.
2. In Claude Code, type `/marginalia` and drop in photos of the pages, or put them in `inbox/` first. No photos? Just say what you read.
3. Answer a few questions. Fragments, voice dictation, and "skip" are all fine.
4. That's it. It's logged, and the important parts will come back to you over the following days and weeks.

## Other things you can say

- **"warm me up for <book>"**: use before you read. You get a quick recall of last time and something to watch for.
- **"rerun"**: review only, for days with no new reading. It counts as a reading day.
- **"light"** / **"deep"**: one question, or a longer conversation.
- **"weekly"**: a recap, three review questions, and next week's lineup.
- **"monthly"**: what went well, what got in the way, and what's next.
- **"long session"**: for a free afternoon, done in chunks with breaks.
- **"start the Harvard Classics plan"**: fifteen minutes a day of the great books.
- **"finished <book>"** · **"add a book: …"** · **"pause <book>"** · **"where am I?"**

## What's in here

| | |
|---|---|
| `profile.md` | You as a reader, in your own words. Marginalia reads it every session. |
| `shelf.md` | Where you are in everything, plus your loose lineup |
| `books/` | One file per book: a running *Previously on…* recap, the people or ideas so far, and every session with your answers |
| `plans/` | Reading plans you're following |
| `commonplace.md` | Lines worth keeping |
| `writing.md` | Writing exercises and experiments |
| `reflections.md` | Weekly and monthly look-backs |
| `inbox/` | Drop page photos and scans here. Processed ones move to `inbox/done/`, which you can empty whenever you like. |
| `review-queue.json` | The spaced-review queue, which appears after your first session (the skill manages it) |

**From an iPhone (on a Mac):** open `inbox` in Finder, Control-click an empty spot, choose **Import from iPhone or iPad → Scan Documents**, and scan the pages. The scan saves straight into this folder.

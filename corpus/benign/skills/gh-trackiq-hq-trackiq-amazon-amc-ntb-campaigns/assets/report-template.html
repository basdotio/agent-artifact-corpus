Method & limits

What these numbers are, and what they are not

Spend is matched by campaign name

AMC's campaign IDs do not match the ad consoles'. Spend was attached by exact campaign name; {{UNMATCHED_NOTE}}

One definition of new-to-brand

Every channel uses AMC's new-to-brand count, so DSP and Sponsored Ads are compared on the same terms. The DSP console's own figure differs.

Seen, not caused

AMC shows which campaign's ad a buyer saw before purchasing. It does not show that the ad made the sale.

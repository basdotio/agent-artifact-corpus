# Video types

Every type is cut as a showreel ([showreel.md](showreel.md)): fast, lit 3D, a new picture every 2 beats. What changes is the story, the chapters and the ending. Offer these in the interview (named after the brand's real products, features and offers), and let the user pick or say "you choose".

Bars assume 140 BPM (a bar is 1.71 s). Each row is 2 bars unless noted. The last chapter always ends on the downbeat so a Reel loops cleanly.

| Type | For | Length | Chapters | Ending | Assets that make it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Product launch** | a new product, app or version | 18 to 25 s | tease (a silhouette or a detail in 3D, lit from the side) → the name slams → 2 or 3 hero features, one per chapter → "available now" | logo build, date or URL, "out now" | product shots or screenshots, the logo, the launch date |
| **Feature spotlight** | one new or overlooked feature | 12 to 18 s | the pain, as a picture → the feature doing it, on the real UI or object → the result, big | logo + feature name | a screen recording or screenshots of the feature |
| **Promo / offer** | a sale, a bundle, a seasonal menu, a booking push (small businesses) | 10 to 15 s | the product, glorified in 3D light → the offer slams (the number the owner gave, never invented) → the conditions in one line → how to get it | logo, CTA (address, link, "DM us"), end date | product photos, the exact offer and its dates |
| **Brand teaser / showreel** | awareness, a new identity, "who we are" | 15 to 20 s | the brand's mark in 3D → 3 values or promises as giant words over abstract 3D built from the mark → the world of the brand (photos or UI) → the tagline | full logo build, tagline | the logo (SVG), the brand book, a few photos |
| **Explainer** (only when asked) | how it works | 30 to 60 s | the explainer column in showreel.md, [story.md](story.md)'s longer shape | logo + CTA | screens of each step |
| **Social proof** | reviews, results, a milestone | 12 to 18 s | the quote or number (real, with its source) slammed → who said it (with consent) → what the product did → CTA | logo + CTA | real reviews or metrics and permission to show them |
| **Event / announcement** | an opening, a webinar, a drop, a hire | 10 to 15 s | what → when (date slams) → where → why come | logo + date + place or link | the date, place, speakers or products |
| **Before / after** | a transformation (a room, a haircut, a dashboard, a workflow) | 12 to 18 s | before, desaturated towards the palette's darkest colour → the product's move (a wipe, a flood in the brand colour) → after, full colour → the one line | logo + CTA | a real before and after pair |
| **Physical product showcase** | a bottle, a shoe, a dish, a device | 12 to 20 s | the product as a lit 3D card or cut-out, orbiting → details in close shots (texture, label) → in use (a photo) → price or CTA | logo + where to buy | a clean product photo (cut-out PNG is best), several angles if possible |

## Rules for every type

- **Real numbers only.** Prices, discounts, dates, ratings and results come from the user, word for word. Never invent one to make a slam land.
- **One message.** Whatever the type, the viewer must be able to repeat it after one watch (three short statements at most).
- **Ending by type.** A launch ends on "out now" and where; a promo on the end date and how to buy; a teaser on the tagline. Hold the ending at least 1.5 s.
- **Series.** When the user makes several videos (a launch, then a feature per week), keep the chapter labels, HUD, music key and ending identical so the series reads as one brand; change the showpiece and the words.

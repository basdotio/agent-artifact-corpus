# Wellness Examples

Use these patterns for nutrition, fitness, sleep, mindfulness, habit, hydration, and self-care apps.

## Prompt: Single Hero

```text
Use the app-store-screenshot skill.
Create a premium App Store portrait screenshot from the attached wellness app screen.
Preserve the app UI exactly.
Use a realistic iPhone frame, a calm branded background based on the screenshot colors, and a short headline above the phone.
Suggested headline direction: make the benefit feel simple, calm, and daily.
```

## Headline Ideas

- "Wellness, made simple."
- "Track what matters."
- "Build better days."
- "Meals, planned fast."
- "Sleep trends that stick."
- "Habits you can keep."

## Visual Direction

Wellness screenshots usually benefit from:

- Soft natural color
- Calm contrast
- Warm or fresh lighting
- Rounded but not childish shapes
- Lots of breathing room

Avoid:

- Harsh medical visuals unless the app is clinical
- Guaranteed claims like "Cure anxiety" or "Lose weight fast"
- Overly busy lifestyle backgrounds

## Multi-Screen Set

Suggested sequence:

1. Daily overview
2. Tracking or logging flow
3. Progress trend
4. Personalized recommendation
5. Reminder, streak, or summary

Keep the headline voice encouraging but grounded.

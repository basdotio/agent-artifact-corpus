# Finance Examples

Use these patterns for budgeting, banking, investing, invoicing, subscriptions, crypto, and expense apps.

## Prompt: Trustworthy Store Screenshot

```text
Use the app-store-screenshot skill.
Turn the attached finance app screenshot into a premium App Store marketing screenshot.
Preserve all UI text, balances, charts, numbers, and layout exactly.
Use a realistic phone frame, a clean trustworthy background based on the app's brand colors, and a concise headline.
Keep the result polished, calm, and credible.
```

## Headline Ideas

- "Your money, clear."
- "Budgets without guesswork."
- "Spending, under control."
- "Invoices without friction."
- "See what changed."
- "Plan with confidence."

## Visual Direction

Finance screenshots usually benefit from:

- Strong legibility
- Crisp contrast
- Minimal backgrounds
- Structured geometry
- Subtle depth rather than decorative effects

Avoid:

- Altering any financial figure
- Adding fake cards, fake balances, or fake transactions
- Overpromising returns, savings, or investment outcomes
- Chaotic neon crypto styling unless the product already uses that language

## Multi-Screen Set

Suggested sequence:

1. Account or dashboard clarity
2. Budget or spend breakdown
3. Transaction detail
4. Forecast, insight, or alert
5. Reports, export, or automation

Keep copy specific and conservative. Trust matters more than hype.

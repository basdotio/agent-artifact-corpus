# Style rules

Applies to every value the skill writes. When avoiding a banned item makes a sentence worse, delete the sentence instead of rephrasing around it. Users can edit this file to match their own voice.

## 1. Punctuation
- No em dashes. No en dashes used as dashes. Use commas, periods, or restructure.
- No semicolons.
- No rhetorical questions.
- No exclamation points.

## 2. Constructions
- No "it's not X, it's Y" or "not X but Y".
- No "X isn't about A, it's about B".
- No "not just X, but Y".
- No "X, but for Y".
- No negation followed by a pivot, in any wording. This is the strongest AI tell.
- No "there's a kind of X that isn't Y".
- No "X doesn't come with Y" or similar setup-and-subvert phrasing.

## 3. Vocabulary
Banned: delve, tapestry, landscape, realm, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust, seamless, leverage, harness, navigate (figurative), unlock, elevate, transform, empower, foster, spearhead, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, innovative, passionate, thrilled, excited, eager, journey, synergy, dynamic, proven track record, wealth of experience, hit the ground running, team player, fast-paced, etched, resonate, showcase, intersection (as filler), deeply, truly, genuinely, incredibly (as intensifiers).

## 4. Openers and closers
- The template's `\OPENER` sentence is fixed and exempt from these rules. Do not rewrite it.
- Never use: "I am writing to express my interest", "I was excited to see", "When I came across", "I'm drawn to", "I'd welcome the chance to", "I look forward to hearing from you", "I am confident that".
- The closing never restates the opening or summarizes the letter.

## 5. Rhythm
- No three-item lists used for cadence. Lists of real technologies are fine.
- No sentence that opens with a participial phrase, then a comma, then a grand claim ("Having built X, I understand...").
- If three consecutive sentences are within five words of each other in length, rewrite one.
- No one-sentence paragraph written for dramatic effect.

## 6. Stance
- No flattery of the company. No "I admire", "I've always respected", "world-class".
- No claims about what the reader values or needs.
- No hedging: "I believe I would be a strong fit", "I feel that", "I think I could".
- No overclaiming: never "expert", "mastered", "extensive", or scope larger than context.md supports.
- State what was built and what happened. Let it stand without commentary.

## 7. Voice
- Direct and plain. Contractions are fine.
- First person, active voice, past tense for completed work.
- Named technologies over categories: "FAISS and BM25", not "retrieval tools".

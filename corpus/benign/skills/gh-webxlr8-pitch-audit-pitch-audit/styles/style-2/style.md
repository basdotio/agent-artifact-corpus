# Style 2: Editorial

Light, paper-toned, magazine feel: serif headlines (Fraunces), the brand colour used sparingly for emphasis, white cards with soft shadows, real photos given room. Same slide layouts and deck-data as style-1, so any deck renders in either style.

Use it for traditional industries, professional services, hospitality and food, or whenever the dark "tech" look feels wrong for the prospect. The theme is generated light from your brand: the accent is darkened just enough to pass WCAG AA on paper.

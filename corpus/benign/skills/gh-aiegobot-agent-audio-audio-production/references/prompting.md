# Audio Prompting Reference

## Sound effects

A strong SFX prompt usually answers:

1. What physically makes the sound?
2. What happens to it?
3. How heavy/large is it?
4. What texture should dominate?
5. What acoustic space is it in?
6. How long should the useful event feel?
7. What must not be present?

Example:

> Heavy steel robot armor struck by an energy projectile, sharp metallic transient followed by a dense low mechanical body and a brief electrical crackle, close and dry, isolated one-shot, no explosion, no voice, no music, minimal tail.

## Ambience

Describe layers and distance rather than asking only for a mood.

Example:

> Night industrial facility ambience: distant ventilation drones, sparse transformer hum, occasional far metallic creak, subtle air movement, no melody, no foreground impacts, stable continuous bed suitable for looping.

## Music

Include useful structural cues:

- genre/style vocabulary;
- tempo or energy;
- instrumentation;
- emotional trajectory;
- duration/section requirements;
- whether vocals are unwanted.

Avoid asking to imitate a living artist or copyrighted recording. Describe musical characteristics instead.

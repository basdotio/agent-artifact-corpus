# Letter of demand (step 2)

Send from the evidence email account to every valid employer address. Attach the invoices (or a
statement of hours and rate). Keep a copy and note any bounces.

Subject: Letter of Demand – Unpaid <Invoices INV-x & INV-y | wages for <period>> ($<amount>)

Dear <manager name> / <EMPLOYER PTY LTD> (ABN <xx xxx xxx xxx>),

I refer to <invoices INV-x ($<amt>, <period>) and INV-y ($<amt>, <period>), both due <date>>.
Copies are attached. Despite my follow-ups on <dates>, they remain unpaid.

Total outstanding: $<amount>

I formally demand payment by 5:00pm <weekday> <date> to the account on the attached invoices,
quoting the invoice numbers.

If payment is not received by then, I will proceed without further notice, which may include a
request for assistance to the Fair Work Ombudsman and a small claims proceeding. I also reserve
all my rights in relation to my employment status, including unpaid wages, award entitlements and
superannuation under the Fair Work Act 2009.

Regards,
<name>
<phone>

---
Checklist before sending: one deadline only (7 days is common); amount matches calculations.md;
no mention of any other grievance; sent from the account that holds the evidence.

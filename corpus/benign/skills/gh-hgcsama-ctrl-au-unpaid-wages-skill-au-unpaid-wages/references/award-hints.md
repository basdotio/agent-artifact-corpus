# Which award usually applies

A candidate only. The user confirms with the FWO Pay and Conditions Tool
(`https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/pay-calculator`) by searching their industry and
occupation; the tool also gives the classification level, casual loading, overtime and penalty
rates. Enterprise agreements can displace an award; the form asks about that.

| Work | Award that usually covers it | Common classification to start from |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse, picking, packing, dispatch | Storage Services and Wholesale Award 2020 | Level 1 / Storeworker Grade 1 |
| Delivery driving, couriers, loading trucks | Road Transport and Distribution Award 2020 | Grade 1 driver / loader |
| Cafe, restaurant, bar, kitchen hand | Hospitality Industry (General) Award 2020 or Restaurant Industry Award 2020 | Food and beverage attendant / kitchen attendant Grade 1 |
| Bubble tea, takeaway, fast food counter | Fast Food Industry Award 2020 (if the shop is mainly takeaway) else Restaurant/Hospitality | Level 1 |
| Retail shop, supermarket | General Retail Industry Award 2020 | Level 1 |
| Cleaning | Cleaning Services Award 2020 | Level 1 |
| Office admin, data entry, handling mail | Clerks—Private Sector Award 2020 | Level 1 |
| Tutoring, education support | Educational Services (Post-Secondary Education) Award 2020 or the relevant school award | varies |
| Hairdressing, nails, beauty | Hair and Beauty Industry Award 2020 | Level 1 |
| Security | Security Services Industry Award 2020 | Level 1 |
| Construction labouring | Building and Construction General On-site Award 2020 | CW/ECW 1 |

Points that change the money, all visible in the Pay Calculator:

- Casual loading (25% in most awards) is on top of the base rate; check whether a quoted hourly
  figure already includes it.
- Overtime after the daily or weekly ordinary-hours limit; weekend and public-holiday penalties.
- Minimum engagement per shift for casuals (often 2 to 4 hours).
- Rates change each 1 July; use the rate for the period worked, not today's.
- Junior rates apply under 21 in some awards.

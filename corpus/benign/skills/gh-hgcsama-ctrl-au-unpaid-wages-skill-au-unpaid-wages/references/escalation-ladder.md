# Escalation ladder, agencies and referrals

## The order, and why

| Step | Template | Purpose | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Chaser | `assets/templates/chase-email.md` | Create a written record that payment was asked for | Polite, short, keep in one thread |
| 2 Letter of demand | `assets/templates/letter-of-demand.md` | Fix the amount, set one deadline, reserve employment-status rights | Send to every valid address; note bounces |
| 3 Final notice | `assets/templates/final-notice.md` | Close the direct channel cleanly | No new deadline; "I will not contact you again"; sent before step 4 |
| 4 FWO enquiry | `references/fwo-my-account-walkthrough.md` | Formal request for help; may lead to Dispute Assistance | Only after the user reads every field |
| 5 Small claims | below | Court order for the money | Get free legal advice before filing |

Each step waits for the previous deadline to pass. Skipping a step is fine if the user has
already done it; repeating a step is not.

## Agencies (confirm numbers on the official sites before calling)

| Need | Who | Contact | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unpaid wages, award rates, sham contracting | Fair Work Ombudsman | 13 13 94; `services.fairwork.gov.au/my-account` | Free; not anonymous when recovering money; 6-year limit on wage claims |
| Interpreter for any call | TIS National | 131 450 | Free for government services; FWO can also arrange |
| Free employment law advice (VIC) | JobWatch | 03 9662 1933; 1800 331 617 regional | Call before deciding employee vs contractor strategy |
| Free legal advice (any state) | Community Legal Centre | search "community legal centre" + suburb | Many have employment clinics |
| Unpaid superannuation | ATO | 13 10 20; "report unpaid super" online | Separate from FWO |
| International students | University student union / student legal service | on campus | Often also handle special consideration for failed subjects caused by financial hardship |
| Workplace safety (separate file) | WorkSafe in the state (VIC: 1800 136 089) | | Never mix with the wage claim |

Other states have equivalents to JobWatch (e.g. community legal centres with employment
programs, or union-run advice lines); search by state.

## Small claims, if FWO cannot resolve it

| Option | Basis | Ceiling | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fair Work small claims, Federal Circuit and Family Court (Division 2) | Employee entitlements under the Fair Work Act / award | $100,000 | Simplified procedure; needs employee status; parties usually bear own costs; filing fee applies |
| State Magistrates' / Local Court civil claim | Contract debt for the invoices | varies by state | Does not need employee status; cannot recover award shortfall or super |

The choice depends on whether the employee argument is strong. Refer the user to JobWatch or a
community legal centre at this point rather than deciding.

## Things to say every time they come up

- "I am not a lawyer; this needs confirming with FWO / JobWatch / a community legal centre."
- "Do not contact the employer again now that the final notice has gone."
- "Back up the chat today."
- "Keep the safety / tax / visa issue in its own file; it must not appear in the wage claim."

# Gotchas — Contract Lifecycle Manager

Add a line each time Claude trips on something new.

---

## Review Gotchas

- **Not legal advice.** This skill structures review and drafts language. Counsel reviews RED items and anything above the user's legal threshold. Say this at the end of every review.
- **Read the schedules and linked terms.** Pricing, SLAs, security and data terms often sit in order forms, DPAs or web URLs outside the main text. List what was not provided as "not reviewed". Never imply full coverage.
- **Whose side?** Liability and indemnity analysis flips depending on buyer or seller. Confirm the side first. Default is buyer.
- **Order of precedence matters.** Check which document wins on conflict (agreement, SOW, order form, supplier online terms). A favourable MSA is worthless if the order form overrides it.
- **Do not invent a playbook.** If the user has no standard positions, say the review uses general commercial standards. Do not present generic positions as theirs.
- **Never alter quoted language.** When showing current clause text, copy it exactly.
- **Jurisdiction changes the answer.** Enforceability of caps, non-competes, auto-renewal and consumer-style notice rules vary by law. Flag "check under governing law" rather than asserting what is enforceable.
- **Redlines must be proportionate.** Ten Tier 1 asks on a low-value, commodity contract wastes goodwill. Calibrate to value, leverage and risk.

## Obligations and Dates Gotchas

- **Calculate the notice deadline, do not copy the expiry date.** The deadline is expiry minus the notice period, adjusted for how notice must be served (written, to a named address, business days).
- **Amendments override the base contract.** Apply them in date order and state which governs. A missing amendment makes the dates unreliable.
- **Evergreen and rolling terms.** No end date does not mean no exit. Look for termination-for-convenience notice instead.
- **Service credits expire.** Many contracts require claims within a short window. Put the claim deadline in the register.
- **Minimum commitments are liabilities.** A shortfall can be payable even if the service was not used. Show the exposure.

## Renewal Gotchas

- **Do not let an auto-renewal pass by default.** Silence is a decision. Make the choice explicit and dated.
- **Switching cost is real.** Compare options on total cost including migration, retraining and risk, not unit price.
- **Incumbent performance data may be thin.** If there is no scorecard history, say the recommendation rests on limited evidence.

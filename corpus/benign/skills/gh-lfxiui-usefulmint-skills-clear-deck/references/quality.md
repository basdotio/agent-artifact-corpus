# Presentation acceptance
- Each slide supports the stated audience decision and has a different job.
- Facts and proposal assumptions are distinguishable. No fabricated charts or market figures.
- Title, diagram and notes agree. Charts have labels, units and a source when data is supplied.
- Open the SVGs at 1600×900 and as thumbnails; inspect all text bounds.
- Source text remains editable. Describe SVG, PDF and PPTX as separate formats and verify each before claiming delivery.

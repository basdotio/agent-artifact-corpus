# Singapore citation practice

## Governing references

Use the **Style Guide for the Singapore Law Reports, 2021 Edition** as the default
editorial baseline, subject to the user's specified publication or house style and
applicable court directions. The **Singapore Academy of Law Style Guide Quick
Reference, 2007 Edition** is a historical comparator, not an equal current default.
Resolve a conflict explicitly. Do not claim the 2021 edition is the latest without
checking. These are distinct publications; do not conflate their titles.

Both user-supplied PDFs were examined on 20 October 2026. They are not redistributed.
The [SMU citation guide](https://researchguides.smu.edu.sg/c.php?g=421764&p=2883161)
links public reading copies of the [2021 SLR guide](https://researchguides.smu.edu.sg/ld.php?content_id=50481427)
and [2007 SAL quick reference](https://researchguides.smu.edu.sg/ld.php?content_id=26900575).
Those university-hosted copies reproduce the SAL publications; the university is not
the rule-making publisher. Consult the guide itself for exceptions and foreign sources.

## Practical checks

| Material | Check |
|---|---|
| Reported case | Verify the case name, year, volume if present, series, first page and pinpoint. Prefer the appropriate SLR/SLR(R) report under the applicable court directions. |
| Unreported case | Use the assigned neutral citation and actual pinpoint. Do not invent a report citation or add an “unreported” label by default. |
| Case pinpoint | Retrieve the complete paragraph(s) in the version cited. Use `at [35]` for one paragraph, `at [35]–[37]` for a continuous range, explicit pinpoints for disjoint paragraphs, and `at [35(b)]` for a sub-paragraph; distinguish report pages. |
| Legislation | Verify the exact instrument and applicable version, then retrieve the specifically cited section/subsection/rule or schedule unit. Preserve its short title, year, edition where applicable and precise locator; historical chapter citations and modern titles need different treatment. |
| Hansard | Record debate date, volume and column if supplied, speaker and capacity, plus a direct link. Use the guide's electronic format where conventional locators are unavailable. |

SLR(R) covers the reissued 1965–2009 reports. Do not obtain a reissue page number by
changing the letters in an older SLR citation. Nor should every SLR(R) citation be
forced into a volume-bearing pattern: historical report formats vary.

Neutral court designators commonly encountered include `SGCA`, `SGHC(A)`, `SGHC`,
`SGHCR`, `SGHC(I)` and `SGHCF`. Preserve parentheses and verify the designator from the
judgment. `SGHC` before 2 January 2021 denotes the former High Court; thereafter
it denotes the General Division. Check the source for other courts' designators.

Apply [paragraph evidence](paragraph-evidence.md) to paraphrases as well as quotations.
During cite-checking, verify the original cited passage before proposing any correction.
For legislation, use [statutory evidence](statutory-evidence.md) and separately record
instrument identity, the version applicable to the facts, and exact provision support.
Recheck these for each fresh draft rather than reusing a prior document's findings.

Do not substitute an SLR citation into a quotation checked only against eLitigation.
The reported text can have editorial differences. If the report is inaccessible,
state that the unreported text was checked and that report comparison is pending.
Do not represent the resulting research copy as a court-compliant authorities bundle.

## Guide sections to consult

These are section identifiers and printed page numbers, not PDF viewer offsets.

| Task | 2021 SLR guide | 2007 SAL quick reference |
|---|---|---|
| Reported cases and pinpoints | 2–1.1; 2–1.1.5 | Consult the corresponding case citation and pinpoint headings; numbering differs. |
| Neutral citations | 2–1.2.1 | Use for historical comparison only. |
| Revised statutes | 2–2.1.2.2, printed pp 39–40 | Predates the 2020 Revised Edition and modern naming conventions. |
| Subsidiary legislation | 2–2.1.3 | Check the historical edition actually cited. |
| Parliamentary debates | 2–3.1, printed p 48 | Compare its government publications guidance. |
| Quotation and emphasis | 1–2.6, especially 1–2.6.3–1–2.6.6 | Compare against the original text and the requested editorial style. |

For statutes, read [the revised-edition protocol](statutes-revised-editions.md) alongside
this guide. AGC's current citation guidance and the actual legislation take priority
over outdated assumptions in an editorial reference.

Modern revised-statute illustration: `Interpretation Act 1965 (2020 Rev Ed) s 9A`.
This illustrates a citation shape; it does not establish which amendments applied
on the research date. Do not replace historical citations globally without checking
the intended version. For short references, use defined abbreviations consistently;
do not automatically convert legislation references into `ibid` or `supra` chains.

In Markdown, italicise case names where appropriate; preserve source spellings and
anonymisation. A hyperlink supplements a citation; it does not replace its legal
identifier. For a style-only correction, do not imply the authority was substantively
verified. For a foreign authority, apply the chosen guide's foreign-source rules
without changing its jurisdiction or legal weight.

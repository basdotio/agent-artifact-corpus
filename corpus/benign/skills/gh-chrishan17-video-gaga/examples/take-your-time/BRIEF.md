# 慢慢说

A worked example of a **long video** (about three minutes). Everything here was written for this brief; the Swiss Kinetic preset contributed only its style (one red on paper and ink, a 12-column grid, snap easing, type slamming out of masks, the beat-step hairline), set in Chinese.

- **Message:** a man who stutters tells how a word gets stuck, how he learned to go round it, and how his sister's wedding toast took eight seconds to start. Nobody laughed; they waited. The ask at the end: if something is stuck in you, say it slowly, someone will wait.
- **The twist:** near the end he says the voice you hear isn't his. The voice is borrowed; every word is his. That is what this tool is for.
- **Audience:** anyone who finds it hard to say what they mean. 16:9, burned-in captions (type that already shows the line keeps its caption in the .srt only).
- **Voices:** him, zh-CN-YunxiNeural at +0 %, set in Noto Sans SC 900. His sister, zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural, set in Noto Serif SC: two voices, two typefaces.
- **Visual system:** the red caret is his voice (it blinks while it waits, shivers when it is stuck); the red slab is the wall. The wall comes back in every chapter: on the street, behind 不行, on the stage. It falls over on 慢慢说 and breaks on his first sentence. The opening typed line returns, still stuck, in the last chapter, and the route that went round the wall now goes straight through it.
- **Score:** intimate, tense, then free · 88 BPM · A minor, opening into C major at the wedding · soft pad and keys, a low bass, a plucked arpeggio and a light kit only when the story moves · celesta melody in the gaps. One part per chapter (below).

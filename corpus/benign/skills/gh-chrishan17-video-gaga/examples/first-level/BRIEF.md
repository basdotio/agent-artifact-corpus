# 第一关
- The line: "终点不插旗子，要抱一下。"
- Angle: a mother tells how her seven-year-old drew her workday as a game level on graph paper, and what the kid put at the finish (also considered: the kid's tally of days cleared, told as a speedrun log; the mother playing the level as a boss fight with her own tiredness)
- Audience and format: anyone who loves pixel games or has a kid; the pixel-retro example of video-gaga · 1920×1080 · ≈ 65 s
- The device: one level drawn in pencil on a 方格本, one graph square = one pixel. When the mother "plays" it, a raster line sweeps the page and the pencil squares boot into Famicom colour; the camera side-scrolls with her through home → puddle → subway → office → boss. At the turn the camera pulls out to the paper page again, and the finish is not a flagpole but a small house with the kid at the door, arms open. Because the story is a child expressing herself in the language she has (games), the picture is literally her drawing becoming a game. The level's clock (TIME 0710 → 1900) and sky carry the day.
- Look: stands on Shigeru Miyamoto's graph-paper sketches for *Super Mario Bros.* World 1-1 and the Famicom/NES side-scroller itself (fixed palette, 1 px = 1 cell, a HUD across the top, flat sky) · takes the cell grid as the only unit, the side-scroll camera, the HUD, the flat NES sky and the pencil-on-graph-paper page as the place the game comes from · this story changes the hero (a mother with a bag and one life), the enemies (commuters with mushroom caps, a boss who breathes deadlines), and the ending (a hug instead of a flag) · palette: paper #F4F1E6, grid #B8CFE0, graphite #4A4A50, then NES #5C94FC sky, #C84C0C brick, #FCA044, #00A800, #FCFCFC, #000000, #F83800, skin #FCBCB0; colour events: act 1 pencil only, act 2 boots into Famicom colour and the sky turns from morning blue to dusk orange to night, act 3 back to paper, where the kid's red crayon is the only colour · light flat (it's a game and a page), one soft daylight from the upper left on the paper scenes · type: Press Start 2P for the game (HUD, 1-1, CLEAR) · Long Cang pencil handwriting for the kid's notes and the hero words (要抱一下 ≈ 300 px, notes 56–72 px) · captions Noto Sans SC 700 in a black NES text box
- Motion: the game moves in whole cells on eighth notes (walk cycles, jumps on parabolic arcs, impacts on twos); the camera chases her with a little lag; the paper scenes move like a hand: pencil fills square by square · signature moves: the raster boot from pencil to colour; the side-scroll that never cuts direction (left → right all film); the pull-out from game to page at the turn; the two sprites closing to one shape
- Voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural −4% (the mother); the kid's two lines zh-CN-XiaoyiNeural −6…−10%, +12 Hz · captions burn (NES text box), the kid's 要抱一下 file-only (it's on screen in her handwriting)
- Score: bouncy, warm, homemade · Lena Raine's *Celeste* (felt piano figure under a chiptune square lead) with a Koji Kondo calypso bounce · 124 BPM F major · theme: C–F–A–G–F, stated on the page piano, in full on the square lead over the montage, home on a music box · form (36 bars): page 0–4 piano alone · boot 5 · verse 6–13 (F Am7 B♭maj7 C7sus, F Am7 Gm7 C7; marimba engine, picked bass, room kit) · bridge 14–15 in D minor for the boss · build 16–17 · theme in full 18–21 (F A7 Dm7–G7 Gm7–C7) · piano alone 22–26 hanging on C7sus · B♭add9 27 · home 28–31 (B♭m6 the one sad colour) · clear 32–35, the last F on today's stroke · meets the picture only at the game starting (bar 6), the flag (22), 要 (27) and the stroke (35) · sounds: the page, the page turn, the pencil stroke

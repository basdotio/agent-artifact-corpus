video-gaga asks a few sharp questions and shows you style frames. It writes the storyboard, then draws every frame in HTML Canvas or three.js and renders frame-exactly. A generated score keeps the cuts on the beat and leaves room for the voice, and free narration and subtitles sync to the word.

01 ASK4–7 questions, each with three concrete options. One is recommended, with the reason.
02 SHOWThree style frames rendered with your real title. You pick one.
03 PLANA storyboard with voice lines and music-only beats, focal points, exits, and the word or beat each hit syncs to.
04 BUILDCanvas or three.js scenes, Edge TTS word timings and a score arranged from the timeline. The agent reviews its own probe frames.
05 VERIFYParallel render and a mix normalised to −16 LUFS. gaga check reports A/V sync, music balance, captions and duration.

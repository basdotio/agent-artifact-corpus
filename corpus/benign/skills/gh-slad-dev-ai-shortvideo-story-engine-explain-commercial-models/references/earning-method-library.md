## Use the library

Identify the primary revenue engine, then supporting engines. Do not list ten labels without explaining which one drives profit and cash.

For each pattern record:

`payer | unit charged | collection timing | variable cost | fixed cost | bottleneck | downside owner`

## Sell ownership

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product margin | sell goods above landed and operating cost | sourcing, design, brand, distribution | inventory, returns, price competition |
| Private label | outsource production, own brand and channel | margin and customer ownership | quality, supplier dependence |
| Made-to-order | collect order before production | low inventory, customization | lead time, delivery failure |
| Preorder/crowdfunding | fund production with committed demand | validates demand and working capital | refund and execution obligations |
| Limited edition/scarcity | restrict supply and raise willingness to pay | status and collectability | artificial scarcity backlash |
| Bundling | combine items for one price | raises basket size, hides comparison | unwanted components and churn |
| Unbundling | sell one high-value component separately | focus and transparent value | smaller wallet share |
| Consumables/razor-and-blade | cheap durable base, recurring refills | installed-base lock-in | compatibility backlash and alternatives |

## Sell labor or outcomes

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hour/day rate | charge for time | simple and low startup cost | income capped by hours |
| Fixed-fee project | charge for defined deliverable | rewards efficiency | scope creep and estimation error |
| Retainer | recurring fee for reserved capacity | predictable revenue | underuse/overuse conflict |
| Outcome/performance fee | share verified savings, sales, or recovery | aligns incentives | attribution disputes and delayed cash |
| Managed service | operate a function for the client | sticky recurring relationship | service quality and labor intensity |
| Agency/broker representation | act on behalf of a buyer or seller | information and negotiation advantage | conflicts of interest |
| Training/coaching | sell capability improvement | high gross margin and reusable content | proof of outcome and reputation |
| Certification/testing | charge to verify competence or compliance | trust and standard-setting | credibility and regulatory scrutiny |

## Sell access and time

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription | recurring payment for continued access | predictable revenue and retention data | churn and content/service burden |
| Membership club | fee for prices, community, status, or privileges | loyalty and cash in advance | weak ongoing value |
| Rental/leasing | charge for temporary asset use | monetize high-cost assets repeatedly | utilization, maintenance, residual value |
| Franchise | upfront and ongoing fees for brand/system access | capital-light expansion | franchisee quality and brand damage |
| Licensing | fee for permission to use IP, brand, process, or technology | scalable with low delivery cost | enforcement and obsolescence |
| Royalty | percentage or unit payment tied to usage or sales | participates in upside | reporting and audit risk |
| Usage-based/API | charge per transaction, request, seat-hour, or unit | aligns price with consumption | volatile bills and demand |
| Time-share/capacity reservation | sell slices of scarce capacity | improves utilization | complexity and customer dissatisfaction |

## Coordinate transactions

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace take rate | percentage or fixed fee per transaction | network effects, asset-light supply | leakage, trust, subsidy cost |
| Listing/lead fee | charge suppliers for visibility or qualified demand | monetizes intent before purchase | low lead quality |
| Escrow/settlement fee | charge to hold and release funds safely | trust and reduced counterparty risk | regulation, fraud, custody liability |
| Payment toll | small fee on high-volume flows | scale and embedded infrastructure | regulation and commoditization |
| Auction | charge seller/buyer fees while price discovers demand | monetizes scarce or heterogeneous goods | manipulation and thin liquidity |
| Aggregator | collect fragmented supply into one searchable interface | convenience and comparison | supplier dependence and disintermediation |
| Group buying | aggregate demand for lower price | viral acquisition and purchasing power | quality, subsidy, fulfillment |
| Logistics/fulfillment fee | charge for storage, delivery, or returns | density and operational moat | capital intensity and service failures |

## Monetize intellectual property and software

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS | recurring software fee | low marginal distribution cost | churn, security, competition |
| Freemium | free core, paid limits/features/status | low-friction acquisition | low conversion and service cost |
| Open-core | open base, paid enterprise/security/hosting | adoption and developer trust | community conflict |
| Developer platform | tools are free/cheap; charge hosting, distribution, or transactions | ecosystem and switching costs | platform governance |
| Content/IP franchise | monetize characters/stories through products, licensing, media, and events | many revenue surfaces | hit dependence and dilution |
| Data/analytics | charge for cleaned data, benchmarks, alerts, or decisions | learning and information advantage | privacy, rights, accuracy |
| Terminal/workflow system | high subscription for must-have information integrated into work | high switching cost | niche concentration |

## Monetize attention and demand

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising | third party pays for audience reach or action | user service can be free | privacy, ad load, platform dependence |
| Sponsorship | brand pays to associate with content/community/event | high-value integrated exposure | trust and disclosure |
| Affiliate/referral | commission for attributed sale or lead | no inventory | attribution and audience trust |
| Influencer commerce | content creates demand, commerce captures margin/commission | low-friction conversion | volatile attention and product quality |
| Loss leader | sell one item below normal margin to acquire traffic | cross-sell and habit | customers buy only the cheap item |
| Cross-subsidy | one segment funds another | rapid adoption and ecosystem growth | hidden dependence and regulation |
| Loyalty program | rewards create repeat purchase and customer data | retention and share of wallet | reward liability and low differentiation |

## Monetize capital, liquidity, and risk

| Pattern | How money enters | Advantage | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interest spread | borrow/fund lower, lend/invest higher | scalable with risk systems | credit, rate, liquidity risk |
| Float | earn on funds held before payout | low-cost temporary funding | legal ownership and withdrawal risk |
| Collateralized lending | lend against recoverable asset value | lower loss severity | valuation and liquidation risk |
| Factoring | buy receivables at a discount | converts seller cash sooner | debtor default and fraud |
| Insurance pooling | collect premiums, pay uncertain losses | diversification and underwriting data | correlated catastrophe and underpricing |
| Guarantee fee | charge to absorb defined default risk | enables transactions | tail loss and moral hazard |
| Asset management fee | percentage of assets, performance, or service fee | recurring base | market dependence and fiduciary duty |
| Market making/spread | quote buy and sell prices, earn spread | liquidity provision | inventory and adverse-selection risk |
| Leasing/finance lease | asset use plus financing return | collateral and recurring cash | residual value and default |

Do not present regulated finance patterns as ordinary side hustles. Verify licenses, custody, disclosure, capital, consumer protection, and suitability.

## Combine models

Common combinations:

- marketplace + advertising + payments + fulfillment;
- hardware + consumables + service subscription;
- franchise + real estate rent + supplier rebates;
- free content + advertising + affiliate + paid community;
- SaaS + usage fee + implementation service;
- membership + low product margins + ancillary services;
- IP licensing + limited products + events + digital content;
- direct retail + supplier terms + negative working capital;
- escrow + transaction fee, without unauthorized use of safeguarded funds;
- subscription + data advantage + workflow switching cost.

When combining, identify conflicts. Advertising may damage subscription trust; marketplace ownership of inventory may compete with sellers; lending to platform merchants may distort recommendations.

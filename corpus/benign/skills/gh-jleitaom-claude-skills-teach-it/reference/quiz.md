# Self-check quizzes

Optional (on by default). 4–6 questions at the end of each part; 10–15 in the
final cumulative quiz.

## Mix of question types
- **Explain it** — "In your own words, why does …?"
- **Predict** — "If you double X, what happens to Y?" (check it in the lab)
- **Read the figure** — a question answered by playing with a specific lab figure.
- **Real world** — "Why did <case study> choose … instead of …?"
- **Apply** — "Would you use <topic> for …? Why or why not?"

## Format (Markdown, answers hidden)

```markdown
### Self-check

1. <question>
2. <question>

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. <answer, with the reason, and the § to reread if wrong>
2. …

</details>
```

If they paste their answers in chat, give feedback point by point and suggest
which section or lab figure to revisit.

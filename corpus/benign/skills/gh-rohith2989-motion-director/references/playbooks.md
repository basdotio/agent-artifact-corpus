# Playbooks — structures that worked

Each playbook is a starting structure, not a template to fill. Adapt it to the subject's real story.

## Launch film (60–90 s; a long keynote cut can run ~2:30 if asked for "all the sections")
Music: 120 BPM deep house in D minor 9, sections on bars (2 s). Titles carry it; no voice.
| Bars | Section | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Manifesto / hook | kinetic type on a dark ground, 3 short lines on downbeats; the mark is born | struck chord + sub per line |
| 4–6 | Title | "Introducing X" — the mark and wordmark; filtered groove | filter sweep opening |
| 6–10 | Hero | the most distinctive single image of what it does (e.g. a wall of real questions answered, flipping between the two places it lives) | FULL groove, hook |
| 10–15 | Demo | the real UI doing the main job end to end (type, send, answer, result) | groove, quieter, UI sounds on top |
| 15–17 | How it works | "Three steps" — chips/steps with literal copy | open hat accent |
| 17–31 | Steps in detail | connect → ask → approve, each a real screen with callouts | groove with hook in places |
| 31–35 | Rhythm break | the product's verbs as huge single words ("Asked. Checked. Answered. Delivered.") | breakdown: struck chord + impact per word |
| 35–56 | Features / outputs / trust | feature cards with live vignettes; documents flying out of files; a night-mode trust bento with a camera tour | groove → reports → breakdown (trust) with a filtered arp |
| 56–67 | Breadth | who it's for (industries), integrations wall (real logos of supported tools) | drop; biggest section |
| 67–70 | Platforms | the same answer in both places it lives | groove |
| 70–76 | Kicker → CTA | "It's more than X. It's Y." → dawn panel, the mark rises, "Start for free" pressed → end card | bed → last FULL phrase → final chord + lockup |
Grammar: every section enters as a panel sliding over the previous (which recedes and rounds). One hero
frame per section. Headlines from the product's own site copy.

## Onboarding / tutorial (45–75 s, narrated)
Music: 100 BPM warm bed under the voice. Captions burned in. A persistent step tracker (4 steps) docks at the
top after the intro and ticks as each step completes.
1. **Welcome** (≈5 s): the mark, "Welcome to X." word-synced; the steps appear on "let's get you set up" and
   dock as the tracker.
2. **Each step = the real screen in a browser window**, the camera leaning into the field being used (≥ 1.6×),
   the cursor clicking on the spoken verb, typing ending on the spoken noun, the field's own help text
   underlined as the voice paraphrases it. Multi-screen steps morph one card through the screens.
3. **Third-party moments** (OAuth consent, another app): the browser navigates (URL changes, load bar), the
   page switches hard when loaded; picking a workspace opens the real picker; "Allow" is pressed on "Allow".
4. **Trust copy** (permissions, passwords): spotlight the sentence being said, dim the rest to ~20 %, zoom in.
5. **The payoff** in the place people will use it (e.g. Slack): question typed with the voice, the answer
   arriving in the thread, its sources underlined on "where every number came from", follow-ups delivering
   files.
6. **Outro**: windows step back, all steps ticked in the centre, "Your new X is ready." → lockup + address.
Check the real product before scripting: removed fields, renamed buttons, and where buttons really lead.

## Feature teaser (15–30 s)
Hook (a question or a bold number, 2–3 s) → the feature working in one continuous camera move (8–15 s) →
the result (a count-up, a before/after) → lockup. One drop at the reveal. Deliver 16:9 and a re-framed 9:16.

## Explainer (60–90 s, narrated)
Problem in the viewer's words (no strawman) → the idea in one sentence → how it works as literal product
steps → one proof (number, customer-agnostic example) → what to do next. Abstract visuals only to bridge into
the product, never instead of it.

## Logo sting (4–8 s)
One gesture that comes from the mark's own geometry (a shape that draws, a light that passes, a character
that blinks), the wordmark letters rising, an address. Sound: a single chord bloom + `lockup_mark`. No
particles exploding, no lens flares.

## Vertical social cut (15–45 s, 9:16)
Re-lay the film for 1080×1920 (stack type, re-frame the camera on the UI's key region), captions above the
bottom safe line, the first second must already show the product or the hook text, end on a 2 s lockup.

## Cutdowns and re-edits of a finished film
- Cut on section boundaries (they are bar lines). Example that shipped: a 2:35 film → 0:00–1:09 + 2:19–2:35
  (85 s): the finale's panel slid over the last flow word via a 1 s bridge rendered on a time-remapped page.
- Re-render only what changed; splice master ranges frame-exactly; regenerate the score on the new timeline;
  remap cues; verify each join (frame difference, clicks, level steps, an AI listener on the join region).
- Re-design a single section on request (e.g. a hero that looked too much like the landing page): render the
  new section's range and splice it in; keep everything else bit-identical.
